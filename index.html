Chapter 03

The 72-Hour Countdown

What to do, in order, once a warning is issued
  1. D-3 · Fuel & cash. Fill vehicles, withdraw small bills, charge power banks.
  2. D-2 · Outside. Clear drains and gutters, store loose furniture.
  3. D-1 · Inside. Move valuables above flood line, freeze water bottles.
  4. D-0 · Go / Stay. Confirm the decision, send the family check-in.
Pro tip: fill bathtubs on D-1 — you can lose water pressure for 3–5 days.
